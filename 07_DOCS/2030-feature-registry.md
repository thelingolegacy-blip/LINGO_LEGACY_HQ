# Lingo ⭐️ 2030 Feature Registry

## Personal intelligence
Ask Lingo, voice, vision, memory, personalization, planning, choices, multimodal creation.

## Life
Health information, adaptive fitness, food planning, hygiene routines, hair/style, wardrobe and daily routines.

## World
Travel, maps/GPS with explicit location permissions, places, restaurants, local services, events and discovery.

## Legacy
Family research, ancestry, burial-location research, graduation/school research, photographs, documents, timelines and story preservation. Sensitive personal information requires permission and source verification.

## Community
Community OS, groups, rides, Loyalty Lane Cycles, challenges, events, moderation, reputation, rewards and local commerce.

## Creator
Video, music, television/series, animation, books, games, studios, production pipelines and Lingo Live human opportunities.

## Commerce
Catalogs, shopping, carts, checkout handoff, orders, fulfillment, merchant portals, loyalty and customer support.

## Reach
Authorized email, messaging, voice and publishing connectors with consent, anti-spam and rate controls.

## Devices
Phone, tablet, computer, wearable, TV, home and vehicle experiences. Device actions are capability- and permission-gated.

## Education
Student, teacher, family and school administration modes; learning and accessibility systems.

## Enterprise
Organizations, teams, roles, policy, audit, billing, security, analytics and support.

## Platform
Cloud, Live Lab, Worker Fabric, Developer SDK, Skills Marketplace, API, webhooks, analytics, QA, security and deployment.
